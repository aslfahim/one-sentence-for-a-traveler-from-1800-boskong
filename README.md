One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — pictured as one
great town square where every soul alive may speak at once, and any stranger
passing by may stop, listen, and answer back, in a square that never sleeps
and never empties.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes that single sentence to
`/output/entry.txt`.
